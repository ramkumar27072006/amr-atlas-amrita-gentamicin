Interpretation boundary. The cohort phenotypes are computationally curated BV-BRC evidence records with laboratory method metadata; they are not independently confirmed experimental susceptibility measurements for this project. This dashboard supports exploratory label-based evaluation and does not establish clinical diagnostic performance or prove real-world gentamicin resistance prediction. AMRFinderPlus outputs are independent determinant evidence, not ML labels or susceptibility ground truth. Bakta annotation and gene-family analyses were not run on this fresh cohort.

Built from saved outputs only. No model, feature extraction, biological annotation, or AMRFinderPlus analysis is triggered by this page.
