How to read this section

The primary dashboard results above remain the sequence-only k=6 baseline. This supplementary experiment compares canonical k=3–8 and additional sequence descriptors. A separate exploratory feature-set sweep also tested AMRFinderPlus determinant indicators. Those outputs are not independent AMRFinderPlus validation and do not replace the sequence-only baseline.

Labels are exploratory BV-BRC phenotype evidence, not independently confirmed clinical susceptibility. The test set contains 47 genomes and was held out from training-time selection.

Interpretation limits

These are exploratory label-based associations. AMRFinderPlus hits support biological interpretation; a hit or no-hit result is not a laboratory susceptibility outcome. Fresh Bakta annotation and gene-family mapping were not run, so no k-mer-to-gene mapping is claimed. The public site remains static: uploaded genomes get local QC and exact saved-cohort lookup only; new-genome model scoring and AMRFinderPlus require a backend.

Interpretation boundary. This dashboard supports exploratory label-based evaluation and does not establish clinical diagnostic performance or prove real-world gentamicin resistance prediction.
